# NumberyardOS Privacy Policy

**Naming:** NumberyardOS is the public-facing name of the application. AvsOS is its internal development name; both refer to the same Reddit Devvit application.

**Effective:** October 21, 2026  
**Contact:** [r/ColoradoAvalanche moderator modmail](https://www.reddit.com/message/compose/?to=%2Fr%2FColoradoAvalanche)

## Who operates NumberyardOS
NumberyardOS is an in-house, noncommercial Reddit Devvit game-day application intended exclusively for r/ColoradoAvalanche and its authorised moderators. The moderators operate its subreddit-facing functionality. It is not offered as a public installable application for other communities. This policy applies to NumberyardOS, not to Reddit or ESPN independently.

## What the app processes
NumberyardOS processes publicly available NHL schedules, scores, play-by-play events, player statistics and related public images; Reddit post and comment identifiers, game IDs, goal-event identifiers, app-generated event status, and moderator-provided game-thread notices. NumberyardOS may read publicly available game-thread content where needed for its game-centre or moderator features. Reddit supplies app/platform context necessary to perform authorised actions.

NumberyardOS does **not** ask users to register for an NumberyardOS account or provide an email address, password, payment information or location. It does not intentionally collect personal profiles, browsing histories, advertising identifiers, precise geolocation or private Reddit messages. The app does not intentionally transmit Reddit usernames, private messages or Reddit authentication tokens to ESPN.

## Why data is used
The data is used to display Avalanche game information, create and maintain game-day threads and text fallbacks, support moderator notices, cache public hockey data, track posted goal events to prevent duplicate automatic comments, and provide optional experimental game analytics. Visual and spoiler controls are app display features; NumberyardOS does not use them to build advertising profiles.

## Where data is stored and shared
Operational state is stored through Reddit Devvit Redis and platform infrastructure. Records may include game-to-post mappings, publicly available game data caches, moderator notices, generated TextCenter comment IDs and goal-event deduplication keys. Some operational records can remain while the app is installed; no fixed deletion schedule is represented here. Information published as Reddit posts or comments is subject to Reddit's public visibility and moderation rules.

The server requests public sports data from **site.api.espn.com**. Sports-related image assets may load from ESPN-operated hosts. Reddit processes activity on its own platform under its policies. NumberyardOS does not sell personal data, run third-party advertising trackers or transfer Reddit content for AI model training.

## User choices and requests
Participation in Reddit game threads is optional. Users can choose whether to open or interact with the NumberyardOS interface. Reddit account, post and comment controls are administered by Reddit. Questions about NumberyardOS operational data or requests for review should be sent through [r/ColoradoAvalanche moderator modmail](https://www.reddit.com/message/compose/?to=%2Fr%2FColoradoAvalanche). Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and available Devvit/Reddit platform controls.

## Security and accuracy
Access to NumberyardOS moderator actions is restricted using Reddit's moderator permissions. Public game data and experimental analytics may be delayed, incomplete or incorrect. We aim to restrict app storage to what is needed for subreddit functionality; no service can promise absolute security.

## Policy changes
We may update this policy when NumberyardOS changes. The current public version and its effective date will appear on this page.
